Part II: Exercises¶

(1) Manual PCA¶

$$ X=\begin{bmatrix}7&7&8&6\\10&2&4&3\\2&7&3&5\\2&5&7&4\\8&5&3&2\end{bmatrix} $$

Standardize $z=\dfrac{x-\bar{x}}{s}$

$$ \bar{x}=\begin{bmatrix}5.8&5.2&5&4\end{bmatrix},\qquad s=\begin{bmatrix}3.6332&2.0494&2.3452&1.5811\end{bmatrix} $$

$$ Z=\begin{bmatrix}0.3303&0.8783&1.2792&1.2649\\1.1560&-1.5614&-0.4264&-0.6325\\-1.0459&0.8783&-0.8528&0.6325\\-1.0459&-0.0976&0.8528&0\\0.6055&-0.0976&-0.8528&-1.2649\end{bmatrix} $$

(a) Covariance matrix

$$ C=\frac{1}{n-1}Z^TZ=\begin{bmatrix}1&-0.5977&-0.1467&-0.4352\\-0.5977&1&0.2601&0.6944\\-0.1467&0.2601&1&0.6068\\-0.4352&0.6944&0.6068&1\end{bmatrix} $$

(b) Diagonalization, sort the order and solve $\det(\lambda I - C)=0$:

$$ \Lambda=\begin{bmatrix}2.4129&0&0&0\\0&0.9624&0&0\\0&0&0.4325&0\\0&0&0&0.1922\end{bmatrix} $$

$$ V=\begin{bmatrix}-0.4522&0.5586&0.6870&-0.1076\\0.5493&-0.3069&0.5208&-0.5769\\0.3971&0.7382&-0.3974&-0.3734\\0.5798&0.2209&0.3145&0.7184\end{bmatrix} $$

Proportion of variance

$$ \frac{\lambda_i}{\sum\lambda}=0.6032,\;0.2406,\;0.1081,\;0.0480 $$

Cumulative: $60.3\%,\;84.4\%,\;95.2\%,\;100\%$.

Choice of components: we keep PC1 and PC2. Together they explain $\dfrac{\lambda_1+\lambda_2}{\sum\lambda}=\dfrac{2.4129+0.9624}{4}=84.4\%$ of the variance, and two components let us plot the items in 2D. (Keeping three would explain $95.2\%$.)

(c) Transform

The eigenvectors were computed from the standardized data, so the feature matrix we multiply is the standardized $Z$.

$$ Z\,V_2=Z\begin{bmatrix}-0.4522&0.5586\\0.5493&-0.3069\\0.3971&0.7382\\0.5798&0.2209\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}1.5744&1.1387\\-1.9164&0.6705\\0.9835&-1.3437\\0.7580&0.0753\\-1.3994&-0.5408\end{bmatrix} $$

Eigenvector signs are arbitrary, so a column with all of its signs flipped is still a correct answer.

Scikit-learn PCA¶

StandardScaler divides by $n$ instead of $n-1$, so the sklearn coordinates are $\sqrt{5/4}\approx1.118$ times the manual ones. The components and explained variance ratios are the same as the manual calculation.

Interpretation

  • Scree plot: PC1 explains 60.3% and PC2 24.1%, so the two components we kept cover 84.4% of the variance. Three components would be needed to pass 95%.
  • PC1 has loadings $-0.45$ for $f_1$ and $+0.55, +0.40, +0.58$ for $f_2, f_3, f_4$, so it contrasts $f_1$ against the other three features. Items 2 and 5 are on the left because they are high in $f_1$ and low in $f_2$–$f_4$. Items 1, 3 and 4 are on the right.
  • PC2 is driven mostly by $f_3$ ($+0.74$), with some $f_1$ ($+0.56$). It separates item 1 (high $f_3$) from item 3 (low $f_3$ and $f_1$).
  • Items 1 and 2 are farthest apart, so they are the most different items. Item 4 sits near the middle on PC2.
  • The $f_2$ and $f_4$ arrows point in a similar direction because those features are positively correlated (0.69). The $f_1$ arrow points away from $f_2$ because they are negatively correlated (−0.60).
